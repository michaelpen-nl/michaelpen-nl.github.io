Teaching Interests

I am passionate about teaching my students several topics. Currently, I have taught in fields of general economics, mathematics, statistics and econometrics, but I am interested to broaden my horizon in teaching to other fields such as labour, health, or other applied fields in economics. In addition, I am also interested in deepen the beforementioned fields.

Currently, I have experience in organizing and teaching tutorials to undergraduate courses. Although I have much fun with teaching undergraduate students, I am thrived to extend this work to teaching graduate courses, giving lectures, and/or coordinating courses. Besides teaching at the university, I have also experience in providing after-class teaching and exam training, both for-profit and voluntarily.
